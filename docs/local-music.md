# Local music

Place supported music files under the Y1 USB-visible `Music` folder. After safely ejecting and returning storage to the player, use **Settings → Scan Music folder** if the library has not refreshed. Local tracks appear with the online catalog and play without Navidrome.

The USB storage cannot be shared with the PC while the player is reading or downloading music. Use **Settings → Share Music over USB**, finish file transfers, safely eject, then return to player mode. The app checks that local paths remain inside the Music folder.
