# Controls

The Y1 wheel browses lists. Press the centre button to open a selection; a double press starts playback, and a hold opens actions for a track, album or playlist. On the Now Playing screen, a centre press switches between volume and seeking; rotate the wheel to adjust after one full turn arms it. The lower hardware button plays or pauses. Four quick centre presses toggle the control lock.

The player menu leads back to Library, Download queue, Settings and Power off. Settings also exposes key lock, screen timeout, brightness, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and cache options. If the controls appear unresponsive, check the lock state and wake the screen.
