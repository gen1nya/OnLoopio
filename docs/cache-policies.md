# Offline downloads and cache policy

Choose a track, album or playlist and use its action menu to download or keep it offline. OnLoopio shows waiting, active, failed and saved tracks in **Download queue**. A new installation starts with an empty cache; it does not automatically redownload music from an old account.

**Settings → Cache policy** controls the audio budget, free-space reserve, idle age and whether old unprotected downloads may be removed automatically. The default policy leaves automatic deletion off. You can protect individual tracks from cleanup or allow them to rotate. A manual cleanup asks for confirmation.

Completed audio lives on the USB-visible Music volume in an account-scoped layout. Partial transfers are never shown as saved tracks. Local music placed by the user under `Music` is separate from OnLoopio's managed downloads.
